# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — October 2026

First release in English.

- Toolpaths for KiCad boards (isolation, holes, outline) and for DXF/SVG parts (engraving, pocket, outline).
- Up to 10 isolation passes; the preview regenerates on its own and shows the isolation width.
- Copper-free zones: draw rectangles on the preview to remove all non-design copper there.
- Ruler in the preview, in millimeters or inches.
- Guided setup: connection, homing, X0 Y0, Z0 with the probe, height map, tool changes.
- Simulation mode, 3D twin of the Genmitsu Cubiko, tutorial with hand videos, and the course exam.
